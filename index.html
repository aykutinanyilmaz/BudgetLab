Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

BudgetLab ("BudgetLab", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the BudgetLab iOS application.

By using BudgetLab, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a BudgetLab account, we may collect:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo (optional)

Budget Information

BudgetLab stores the financial information you voluntarily enter into the application, including:

  • Budgets
  • Income
  • Expenses
  • Categories
  • Transactions
  • Financial Goals
  • Other budgeting information you choose to create

Your financial information is used solely to provide the functionality of BudgetLab.

2. Authentication

BudgetLab uses Firebase Authentication to securely authenticate users. Authentication data is processed according to Google's privacy practices.

3. Profile Photos

If you upload a profile photo, it is securely stored using Firebase Storage. Your profile photo is associated only with your account and is not publicly accessible.

4. Analytics

BudgetLab uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the application is used and to improve the user experience.

Analytics may include anonymous information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Feature usage
  • Session duration
  • Crash and performance information

We do not use Firebase Analytics to analyze your personal financial information.

5. Premium Subscription

BudgetLab offers optional premium subscriptions using Apple In-App Purchases, managed by RevenueCat.

Payment information is never collected or stored by BudgetLab. Payments are securely processed by Apple. RevenueCat only receives the information necessary to validate and manage your subscription.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide budgeting features
  • Synchronize your data across devices
  • Store your profile photo
  • Improve application performance
  • Analyze anonymous usage statistics
  • Manage premium subscriptions
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud

7. Data Storage

BudgetLab securely stores your information using Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage

Industry-standard security measures are used to protect your information.

8. Sharing Your Information

BudgetLab does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share limited information only with trusted service providers required to operate the application, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple

These providers process your information solely for providing services related to BudgetLab.

9. Data Retention

Your information is retained while your account remains active. If you request deletion of your account, your personal data will be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period unless we are legally required to retain certain information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws (including GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

11. Children's Privacy

BudgetLab is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will promptly delete it.

12. Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Third-Party Services

BudgetLab relies on the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Analytics
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple In-App Purchases

Each third-party provider processes data according to its own privacy policy.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective immediately after the updated version is published. The "Effective Date" at the top of this document will always indicate the latest revision.
